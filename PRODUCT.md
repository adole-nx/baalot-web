# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three distinct audiences:

1. **Student voter in Abuja / Lagos** — first-time digital voter, using a smartphone, wants to feel empowered and safe. Skeptical after decades of election manipulation. Needs the process to feel credible, modern, and accessible.

2. **University rector / election committee head** — institutional decision-maker across Nigerian and pan-African universities. Needs trust signals: compliance, auditability, zero-dispute history. Responds to authority and precision.

3. **Investor / partner in London or Lagos** — evaluating Baalot as a category-defining infrastructure play. Needs to see technical credibility, market size, and a brand that could scale to government elections.

## Product Purpose

Baalot is a blockchain-secured, AI-verified election management platform built for universities, student unions, NGOs, and organizations across Africa. It makes democratic processes tamper-proof, remote-accessible, and auditable. Success means a university running their first election on Baalot and having zero disputes — and then running every future election on Baalot.

## Brand Personality

Cryptographic. Civic. Charged.

A platform at the intersection of African political energy, Ethereum's dark mysticism, and Swiss electoral design. It doesn't apologize for being technical; it makes technology feel trustworthy and powerful.

Emotional target per audience:
- Student voter: "I feel safe. My vote counts. This is real."
- Rector: "This is the infrastructure our institution deserves."
- Investor: "This is category-defining."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: Webflow templates, Notion-style cream backgrounds, soft gradients, "streamline your workflow" copy.
- Fintech navy-and-gold institutional stiffness (KPMG, Deloitte style).
- African-print-as-design shortcut: using Kente-colored buttons or Adinkra symbols as decoration without substance.
- Web3 hype aesthetics: floating 3D coins, rainbow token gradients, "to the moon" energy.
- Student-union budget aesthetic: Comic Sans equivalents, clip art voting boxes, poor contrast.

## Design Principles

1. **Precision signals trust.** Every pixel — the spacing, the data typography, the animation timing — communicates that this platform handles things that matter. Sloppiness is a broken promise.
2. **Data is the design.** Vote counts, turnout percentages, block hashes, ZK proofs — these are not UI elements to hide or soften. They are the product. Celebrate them with monospace precision.
3. **One idea per fold.** Don't rush the story. Each scroll section earns exactly one revelation. The user should feel the weight of each claim before the next arrives.
4. **African context, global quality.** This platform is proudly built for Africa — not as a limitation, but as a starting point for global democratic infrastructure. The design reflects that confidence.
5. **Amber as ember.** The primary accent (ballot amber) should feel like heat, democracy, urgency, and light in a dark system — not like a warning or a caution sign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum; aim for AAA on body text
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must disable all scroll-triggered and entrance animations (instant transitions only)
- All interactive elements keyboard-accessible with visible focus rings
- Color contrast minimum 4.5:1 on all body text against backgrounds
- Mobile-first: primary voter audience is on smartphones
- Multi-language ballot builder referenced in copy — site copy is English but must be structurally ready for i18n
